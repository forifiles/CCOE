About the College

A Distinctive Quality-Based Tertiary Educational Institution

City College of Education, Mararaba (CCoE) is strategically located at No. 1 City College Road, Off Old Karu Road, Sani Abacha Road Estate, Mararaba-Karu, Nasarawa State.

Operating in full alignment with the New NCCE Minimum Standard (2026) and in affiliation with Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko (AAUA), Ondo State for its Degree and PGDE programmes, CCoE combines pedagogical mastery with modern computing, AI & Robotics education, and hands-on entrepreneurship.

✓ Nigeria Certificate in Education (NCE): 7 Specialized Academic Schools
✓ Degree & PGDE (AAUA Affiliation): Accredited University Teacher Education
✓ Education for Self Employment: Dedicated Vocational & Digital Skills Centre

Our Vision, Mission & Philosophy

Our Vision

To be a distinctive quality based educational institution, making the difference in the Nation history through positive impact of its services and its graduate output.

Our Mission

To establish and maintain a most suitable academic environment synergizing world class human capital best technology for creating and impacting knowledge to develop and produce superior entrepreneurial teachers for the Nigeria society.

Our Philosophy

Guided by our motto — "Education for Self Employment" — we train educators who are intellectually grounded, technologically fluent, and entrepreneurially self-reliant.

Core Institutional Values

What Guides City College of Education, Mararaba

  • Academic Excellence: Upholding the New NCCE Minimum Standard (2026) and AAUA University Senate benchmarks.
  • Entrepreneurial Self-Reliance: Equipping every graduate with practical vocational and digital skills ("Education for Self Employment").
  • Technological Innovation: Integrating AI & Robotics, Data Science, Cybersecurity, and Educational Technology.
  • Integrity & Character: Moulding ethical, disciplined educators who lead by example across Nigeria.

Institutional Background

City College of Education, Mararaba (CCoE) is a reputable tertiary teacher-education institution located in Mararaba-Karu, Nasarawa State, serving the Federal Capital Territory corridor and the entire federation.
